## Notes for a Project Manager
The "75% practical" framing signals that this week produces tangible output, not just concepts — useful when justifying the time investment to a team or to your own calendar. As a PM, treat the pre-reading/in-class split as a planning pattern: front-load the conceptual learning so the synchronous time is reserved for building. Knowing the scope is "APIs, automation, integration" helps you set expectations with stakeholders about what your team will actually be able to deliver after Week 4 (working automations, not production systems).

## Notes for a Project Manager
Beyond the motivational message, the practical PM observation here is the metadata inconsistency: the closing slide's date ("March 2026") disagrees with the title slide ("May 2026"). Small version/date mismatches like this are exactly the kind of detail that erodes trust in a document and are worth catching in review. As a PM, treat consistent versioning and dating across an artefact as part of its definition of done — it is cheap to fix and signals overall quality control.

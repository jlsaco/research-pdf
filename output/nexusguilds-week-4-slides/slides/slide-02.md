# Slide 2 — Welcome to Module 2: The Workshop

## What this slide says
Having finished as an AI *user*, learners now enter Module 2 ("The Workshop"), which takes them from user to AI *integrator* — connecting AI to real workflows through APIs, automation, and agents. The slide introduces the motivating story: Andrés's team has 15 manual processes to automate (classifying emails, summarising meetings, screening applications, drafting posts, routing tickets), but the team's skills vary wildly — 3 people write Python fluently while 5 have never coded. His boss asks him to "find a strategy that works for everyone." The takeaway is that automation is a spectrum, not a single skill. The slide also flags an ACTION REQUIRED: create free Zapier and Make accounts and have a Gmail/Google account ready.

## Key concepts
- Module 2 shifts learners from AI user to AI integrator.
- Integration happens through APIs, automation, and agents.
- The "15 Automations Challenge" frames the week with a realistic mixed-skill team problem.
- Core insight: automation is a spectrum, suiting different skill levels — not one skill.
- Prerequisites: free Zapier account, free Make account, and a ready Gmail/Google account.

## Notes for a Project Manager
The Andrés story is essentially a resourcing-and-capability problem dressed as a teaching device — exactly the kind of situation a PM faces. The lesson "find a strategy that works for everyone" maps directly to how you assign automation work: route simple, high-value tasks to no-code tools your non-technical team members can own, and reserve scarce engineering time for the genuinely complex pieces. The "automation is a spectrum" framing gives you vocabulary to triage a backlog of manual processes by skill level required. Practically, ensure your team completes the account setup before any working session — missing prerequisites is the most common cause of a wasted hands-on hour.

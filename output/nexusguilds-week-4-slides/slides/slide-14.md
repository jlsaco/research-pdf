# Slide 14 — Week 4 Deliverables

## What this slide says
This slide names the two required Week 4 deliverables and situates them in the cumulative course portfolio. The first is a Working Automation: a functional Zapier or Make automation following trigger → AI step → delivery, submitted as a screenshot or share link plus a note on which tier was used and why. The second is a Process Map: 5-10 processes mapped to Tool Spectrum tiers with justification, which feeds into Week 5's agent design. Below, a "Your Portfolio: 6 Artefacts" row shows the running collection — W1: Compass, W1+: Playbook, W2: Selection Matrix, W3: Prototype, SS2: Knowledge Guide, and W4: Automation + Map highlighted as the new addition.

## Key concepts
- Two concrete, assessable outputs: a working automation and a process map.
- The automation submission must include rationale (which tier and why), not just the artefact itself.
- Deliverables are cumulative — Week 4 adds the sixth artefact to a growing portfolio.
- The Process Map is explicitly forward-looking, serving as raw material for Week 5.

## Notes for a Project Manager
Think of this as the week's definition of done. Both deliverables pair an artefact with a justification, which is a healthy pattern to carry into your own work: ship the thing, and document the reasoning behind the choice. The "6 Artefacts" portfolio view is a clean example of incremental, visible progress — each week produces a tangible, named output that builds on the last. As a PM, the portfolio framing is worth borrowing: tracking deliverables as a cumulative set makes progress legible to stakeholders and makes it obvious when a week's output is missing or incomplete.

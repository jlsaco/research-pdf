# Slide 15 — Looking Ahead: Week 5

## What this slide says
This slide bridges to Week 5 using the Andrés narrative: his automations are running — emails classified, meetings summarised — but they are isolated, with Marketing's content pipeline not talking to Sales' lead qualification. The metaphor: individual automations are like individual musicians; Andrés needs an orchestra. Next week covers chaining AI steps into multi-step workflows, designing agents that make decisions autonomously, and mapping cross-departmental AI workflows — the point where "AI integration becomes AI orchestration." The Week 5 preview, titled "From Automation to Orchestration," lists three topics: multi-step workflows chaining AI processes together; autonomous agents that make decisions and take actions; and cross-departmental AI workflow mapping. A footer reminds students to bring their Process Map from Exercise 4.C, as it is the raw material for Week 5.

## Key concepts
- The conceptual leap from isolated automations to orchestrated, connected workflows.
- The "musicians vs. orchestra" metaphor framing orchestration as coordination across parts.
- Three Week 5 themes: multi-step chaining, autonomous decision-making agents, and cross-departmental mapping.
- Continuity from Week 4: the Process Map (Exercise 4.C) is the explicit starting input for Week 5.

## Notes for a Project Manager
The orchestra metaphor maps directly onto a familiar PM challenge: you can have many individually working pieces that still fail to deliver value because they do not connect across teams. The Marketing/Sales disconnect is a classic cross-functional handoff gap. The shift from automation to orchestration is the shift from optimising single tasks to coordinating end-to-end, cross-departmental processes — which is fundamentally a program- and process-management concern. Note the continuity discipline too: Week 4's output is deliberately the input to Week 5, so as a PM you would ensure the Process Map is complete and good enough to build on, not just submitted.

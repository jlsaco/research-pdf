# Slide 3 — Week 4 Overview

## What this slide says
This slide lays out the week's roadmap in two halves. Part 1 is the ~1.5h pre-reading and is conceptual: What Is an API?, Five Integration Patterns, The Tool Spectrum (4 tiers), and Anatomy of an AI Automation. Part 2 is the 2h in-class session and is practical: 4.A Three Ways to Automate (demo), 4.B Build Your First Automation, 4.C Process Mapping Workshop, and a preview of Week 5's agents and orchestration. The week is again emphasized as 75% practical.

## Key concepts
- Part 1 (pre-reading, conceptual): API basics, integration patterns, tool spectrum, automation anatomy.
- Part 2 (in-class, practical): three exercises (4.A demo, 4.B build, 4.C process mapping) plus a Week 5 preview.
- The conceptual half front-loads foundations so the synchronous half can be hands-on.
- Reinforces the 75%-practical balance.

## Notes for a Project Manager
This is the week's work-breakdown structure. As a PM, read it as a dependency chain: the four pre-reading concepts are prerequisites for the three in-class exercises, which in turn feed the Week 5 work. The clean split between "learn the concepts asynchronously" and "build together synchronously" is a reusable meeting-design pattern — keep expensive group time for the activities that genuinely need collaboration (building, mapping) and push solo learning to pre-work. Use the four pre-reading topics as a checklist to confirm your team is actually ready before the practical session.

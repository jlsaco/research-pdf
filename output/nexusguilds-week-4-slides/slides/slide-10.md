# Slide 10 — Session Plan

## What this slide says
This slide lays out a minute-by-minute agenda for the 2-hour in-class session, color-coded by conceptual versus practical blocks. It opens (0:00-0:10) with the "15 Automations" story and a Self-Study 2 debrief, then 0:10-0:25 covers core teaching on API fundamentals, the Tool Spectrum, and integration patterns. The practical core runs from 0:25 to 1:50: Exercise 4.A (Three Ways to Automate via Zapier / Make / cURL, 0:25-0:50), Exercise 4.B (Build Your First Automation, trigger → AI → delivery, 0:50-1:30), and Exercise 4.C (Process Mapping Workshop on 5-10 processes, 1:30-1:50). The session closes (1:50-2:00) with a conceptual preview of Week 5's agents and multi-step orchestration.

## Key concepts
- A timeboxed agenda that allocates roughly 65 of the 120 minutes (0:25-1:30) to the two core build exercises.
- Each block is tagged Conceptual or Practical, making the 75%-practical promise concrete and visible.
- The flow moves from story → teaching → guided demo (4.A) → independent build (4.B) → mapping workshop (4.C) → forward preview.
- The agenda mirrors the deck's arc: ground the ideas first, then build, then map work to tiers, then look ahead.

## Notes for a Project Manager
This is essentially a run-of-show, and it is the most directly reusable artefact on the deck for a PM. The explicit time ranges and Conceptual/Practical tags give you a facilitation template you can lift for any workshop you plan: front-load context, protect the largest block for hands-on work, and reserve a short closing slot for next-steps. Watch the two longest blocks (4.A at 25 min, 4.B at 40 min) as the schedule's risk points — if a demo or build runs over, the Process Mapping workshop (4.C) is the segment most likely to get squeezed, and it is the one that feeds Week 5. Plan a buffer or a fallback so 4.C is not sacrificed.

## Global summary

Week 4 of the Nexus Guilds "AI Foundational" course — "The Interface: APIs, Automation & Integration" — teaches working professionals how to connect AI to real workflows via APIs and no-code/low-code automation tools (Zapier, Make, code), framing automation as a spectrum of four tiers rather than a single skill. Anchored by Andrés's "15 Automations Challenge," the deck moves a mixed-skill cohort from AI *user* to AI *integrator*. Part 1 (pre-reading, ~1.5h) builds the concepts — what an API is, five integration patterns, the four-tier tool spectrum, and the Trigger → AI → Delivery anatomy — while Part 2 (in-class, 2h, 75% practical) is hands-on: building the same automation three ways, building your own, and a process-mapping workshop. It closes with deliverables, a portfolio recap, and a preview of Week 5's leap from automation to orchestration.
